# Retention policy planning

The deterministic retention planner verifies an Ed25519-signed, independently supplied exact catalogue digest and installation identity, with a 24-hour catalogue freshness limit. Default retention 30 days and at least three complete, independently restore-tested and off-site-verified copies. Always protect the latest sequence, legal holds, active/incomplete/unverified copies and minimum verified recovery copies. Remaining expired archives are eligible for operator review, not deleted. Fail closed on malformed, stale, duplicate or mismatched records. All existing archives, including eligible candidates, block retirement of their referenced encryption keys. No credentials, real archive deletions or external storage operations. Deployment owner must maintain a complete trustworthy catalogue, independently verify off-site copies and restore evidence, protect legal holds, reconcile actual deletion and operate any scheduled pruning only after explicit approval. The initial default is an engineering policy, not a legal retention determination. A compromised trusted catalogue custodian can misrepresent facts; separate governance and audit are required.
