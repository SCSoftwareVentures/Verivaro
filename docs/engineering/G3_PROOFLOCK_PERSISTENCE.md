# ProofLock persistence (0040)

Milestone schedules are immutable, expected-revision-controlled and explicitly confirmed by an existing authorized project submitter. Unknown dates have no local time or offset. Both PostgreSQL and the Node planner round-trip IANA timezone/offset values; PostgreSQL also stores the original UTC instant, and the planner refuses a divergent stored instant. Window rules reference the exact approved criterion and a scoped milestone, with one immutable proposal and independent decision per criterion version/milestone. No past decision is overwritten. Server-generated timestamps, bounded cardinalities, project locks, no direct service DML, archived-project write denial and scoped consistent read are inherited or enforced. The read returns the assessment, schedule history and window records in one stable database statement, then applies the deterministic planner. The plan flags whether the window was approved by each historical revision end; it does not prove historic evidence capture.

The decision to allow existing submitters to confirm schedules follows current project role policy. Window approval must be independent of both proposer and criterion author. Changing a lead or risk requires a fresh criterion revision and review; an independent window amendment workflow, historical captured-at proof, gap prioritization, alerts and recovery exceptions remain outstanding. No business calendars, payment entitlement or legal conclusions. Scheduling is advisory and does not grant release permission. Live PostgreSQL tests and restore hash checks are added; results must be verified through CI.
