# Preservation lifecycle and portable archive foundation

Exact project-scoped, append-only retention policies (explicit UTC retention date and backup target), immediate holds, owner release requests and independent reviewer approval/denial. Archived project required for disposal planning; policy must exist and date must have elapsed; active holds block requests/approval. New hold or policy invalidates a previous disposal approval even if a hold is subsequently released. Explicit cancellation and stale sequence protections. No destructive execution, no statutory deadline computation, no change to original immutability or existing archival share revocation. Retention target is configured metadata, not evidence of actual backup removal. Lifecycle decisions are the 36th selected TrustProof source type.

Owner-only archived project portable preservation copy uses bounded existing report, current assessment and lifecycle history plus every indexed encrypted-store original (up to 64, 16 MiB originals, 20 MiB archive). Deny legacy files/oversize rather than silently omit them; re-read inventory and authorization at end. Canonical ZIP STORE, fixed paths, SHA-256 index and offline verifier with independently supplied archive digest and scope. Local download is plaintext; protect it. Not an approved handover, full historical database backup, signed archive, import/restore, or proof of delivery. Full graph history, all other module history, large archives, irreversible deletion, derived/file/share cleanup, signed external deletion ledger and restoration suppression remain outstanding.
