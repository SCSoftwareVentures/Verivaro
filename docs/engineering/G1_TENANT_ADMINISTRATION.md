# Tenant administration (0031)

Separate trusted tenant operator login bootstraps a tenant and its initial provider-bound owner atomically. Operator must verify identity and issuer out of band; case UUID correlates external approval, it is not cryptographic identity proof. An owner with a live provider-bound session requests exact provider issuer/subject SHA-256 and a non-owner role; dedicated operator independently verifies the subject and approves within 15 minutes while the original owner's session and generation remain current. Existing active bindings may be reused across tenants, never rebound. No email matching or auto-enrollment. New members do not receive projects automatically. No external IdP account creation, invitation delivery or real provider configured. Runtime never has bootstrap or completion permission. Tenant directory and role changes require current owner. Role changes or revocation withdraw existing project access and rotate generations; no implicit restoration of revoked tenant membership. Owner cannot self-demote or change other owners by ordinary access. Transfer requires current owner request, distinct provider-bound active member acceptance, and dedicated operator completion referencing independently reviewed case; all initiating/accepting sessions and membership generations rechecked, expiry 15 minutes. Copies outgoing owner's active project-owner assignments and revokes all outgoing tenant/project access atomically. Existing codes/approvals tied to old generations invalidated; other owners unaffected. Projects already archived and owned by the outgoing owner block transfer pending dedicated historical custody handover rather than weakening archive invariants. No account deletion or global disable for tenant revocation. Receipts/requests/acceptances append-only, backup preserved. Caps 1024 tenants, 200 historical memberships per tenant and 32768 requests/receipts globally. Browser owner/member controls expose scoped directory, enrollment requests, role changes, succession nomination and acceptance. Bootstrap, independent enrollment approval and transfer completion are intentionally operator-only service adapter actions, with no HTTP route or operator credential in the application runtime. Real provider provisioning remains separate work. Restore quarantine blocks new privileged administration. CI exercises real PostgreSQL role boundaries, repeat request, invalid approval, stale membership generation and concurrent completion; synthetic identity sessions, not live IdP enrollment.
