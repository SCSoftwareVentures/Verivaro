# Immutable OCR transcription correction and independent attestation

Adds a scoped append-only correction history for existing unreviewed OCR-derived requirements. An author can submit a corrected transcription and explanation tied to original PDF/PNG evidence SHA-256, original OCR quote SHA-256, and original OCR region digest. The original criterion and raw OCR text remain immutable. Up to four revisions are allowed and only following independent rejection; pending/approved corrections cannot be silently overwritten. Reviewers must hold independent reviewer permissions and cannot be the requirement author, correction author or original uploader. Corrections cannot modify already reviewed versions or archived projects. Graph assessment shows corrected criterion *only after* independent approval, retains the raw OCR text and complete correction status, and blocks completion while correction is pending or rejected. Standard OCR requirement approval is blocked until the most recent correction has been approved; an independent OCR criterion review still follows. Submission graph stamps include the correction history only when present to retain historical stamps for unaffected projects. Both correction and review are incorporated into TrustProof's append-only source chain and verifier allowlist. No OCR confidence is treated as probability or proof of correctness. New consented UI controls distinguish correction proposal, independent correction review and requirement approval; no external deployments or purchases. Tests cover rejection, retry, tenant role, provenance, idempotency, and TrustProof integrity. General arbitrary mixed-content PDF conversion remains outside the ingestion allowlist pending a separated sandboxed sanitizer.
